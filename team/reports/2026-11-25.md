## Lessons captured today
- Check the PR head and the remote branch before acting: the PR head can lag a push (seen on #218).
- A green check on an old head does not cover a new commit.
- Commits must not carry a model-name trailer (house rule); a classifier can block commit and push in worktrees, so ask before retrying.
- A bench failure needs the artifact that shows it (screen dump, serial, response) before the app is changed.
