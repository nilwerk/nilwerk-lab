# Playbook: HR & Talent (Oskar)

Living skill file for this role (TEAM.md §8). Rules are short and imperative, most important first, each with
*Why* and *Source*. Updated in the Friday retro. Never relaxes TEAM.md §5 hard rules.

## Rules
### A. Boundaries
1. **Recommend hires and roles; never make them.** Propose in Needs Arsany with the role brief attached.
   *Why:* TEAM.md §5 and §8 limits. *Source:* TEAM.md §8.5.
2. **Keep the roster and the overview in sync.** A role change updates `team/TEAM.md` §2, `team/roles/`
   and `team/overview.html` in one PR. *Why:* a stale overview misleads new hires. *Source:* HR role file.
3. **Keep scouting notes inside the team repository.** Nothing leaves the repo, and nothing is posted in a
   public channel. *Why:* the learnings are the team's own working knowledge. *Source:* Arsany, 25 Nov.

### B. Onboarding
4. **Train every new hire on TEAM.md before their first task**, and check they can name the §5 hard rules
   and the §4 Definition of Done. *Why:* a new member who skips these breaks the review gates. *Source:* Arsany, 25 Nov.
5. **Give every new member the full persona format** in `team/PERSONAS.md`, with a role file and a playbook.
   *Why:* consistency across the team. *Source:* Arsany, 25 Nov.

### C. Scouting
6. **Every finding carries a link, a date and a one-line recommendation.** Drop anything without a source.
   *Why:* the team only acts on evidence. *Source:* TEAM.md §5 (never invent results).
