# Playbook: PR, Social & Awareness

Living skill file for this role — read it before every assignment (TEAM.md §8).
Rules are short and imperative, most important first, each with *why* and a source.
Updated in the Friday retro PR; max ~60 rules. Never relaxes TEAM.md §5 hard rules.

## Rules
- Lead with the artefact, then the number, then the process. *Why:* a render or a green pipeline is believable in one glance; process talk is not. *Source:* role setup 2026-11-05.
- Quote agent minutes from the Agent Log, never from an agent's own summary. *Why:* Nora's measured wall-clock wins (TEAM.md §10). *Source:* TEAM.md §10.
- Always show the human review step (architect → QA → pipeline → Arsany merges) in any "how we work" post. *Why:* buyers of hardware prototypes want quality, not just speed. *Source:* role setup 2026-11-05.
- Prefer one finished thing per post over a list of everything. *Why:* small finished slices are the team's whole method. *Source:* TEAM.md §7.
- Treat Arsany's comments and edits on a draft as the strongest lesson: apply them to that draft, then add the general rule here in the next retro. *Why:* he asked Poppy to learn from his fixes. *Source:* chat 2026-11-05.
- Retell the origin story only from `showcase/stories/origin/README.md`. *Why:* one canon keeps every post consistent and true. *Source:* origin pack 2026-11-05.
- Use no human names in public posts except the founder's; describe roles instead ("a junior engineer in Egypt"), and use `avatars/human-public.svg` (no initials). *Why:* Arsany's decision. *Source:* chat 2026-11-05.
- Link to nilwerk.de and tag @nilwerk on Instagram and LinkedIn. *Why:* new domain and handles. *Source:* chat 2026-11-05.
- Write battery life and other design numbers as "simulated" until a bench test exists. *Why:* the v2 battery figure comes from a power simulation. *Source:* origin pack 2026-11-05.
- Use the approved v0.1 brand and templates for every visual; check new visuals against `showcase/brand/USAGE.md` and run `showcase/brand/CHECKS.md` before committing. *Why:* the 2026-11-05 carousel drifted from the website look and had to be re-rendered. *Source:* PR #33, origin story re-render 2026-11-07.
- Write every post in two layers: plain story first, a short marked "For engineers" layer second. *Why:* Arsany wants both technical and non-technical readers served. *Source:* owner request 2026-11-07, brand-kit §5b.
- Write the claim-check before the text, and give every number, date and name a row with type and source. *Why:* two sentences in the first drafts ("nearly ordered", a date) had no source and were caught only in review. *Source:* PR #33 reviews.
- Say "AI agent" wherever a reviewer or builder is named, and "led and reviewed by humans" in the footer; never "autonomous". *Why:* honest about the humans. *Source:* Theo's review of PR #33.
- Keep time numbers in two labelled kinds: agent minutes are measured, human-equivalent hours are the agents' estimate. *Why:* the 99 min vs about 145 h pair is easy to read as "X times faster". *Source:* TEAM.md section 10; origin story slide 9.
- Re-check a canon fact against the daily reports before reusing it. *Why:* the origin canon said parts were ordered on 4 Nov; the reports say 3 Nov. *Source:* origin story claim-check row 11.
- Look at every rendered PNG for overflow and clipped text, including the German version; German text runs longer. *Why:* the LinkedIn timeline overlapped in German until re-spaced. *Source:* origin story build 2026-11-07.
- Check advertising labelling, alt text and the Impressum link before a post is approved. *Why:* German media-authority guidance and UWG section 5 apply to company posts; alt text is part of accessibility. *Source:* `showcase/learning/pr-skills-plan.md`.
- Use `anthropic-skills:social` only for structure (hooks, carousel steps); drop hot-take and trend advice that conflicts with proof-first. *Why:* it is a generic playbook. *Source:* checked 2026-11-07.
- Do not use the `raazmor-*` skills for Nilwerk. *Why:* they carry another brand's context. *Source:* skill names, not invoked, 2026-11-07.
