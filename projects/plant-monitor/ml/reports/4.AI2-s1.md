# 4.AI2 slice 1: baselines on real Mendeley data

Mira (Firmware + Edge AI), 2026-11-07. Reproduce: `scripts/fetch_mendeley.sh`, then `python -I scripts/run_baselines.py data/raw/ex <out>` (seed 0, real data only, no synthetic faults, splits and ignore pad 4 as in the data card). **No model is trained here.** These are baselines and an evaluation harness. Traces to FR-E01, FR-E02, A-032.

## 1. Enforcing mask (A-032)
`evaluate.wetting_usable(df) = lbl_wetting_pos | lbl_wetting_neg`. Ignore-mask ticks (generic wetting +-4 ticks, all of sector 5) are never usable; the function raises if the three columns do not partition the ticks or if sector 5 carries a positive. Every precision/recall count goes through it (`wetting_precision_recall`). Tests include a mutant that adds ignore ticks to `usable` (killed).

## 2. Model A (thirst forecast, FR-E01): average-interval baseline
Target: hours until moisture falls below T = 25 (0..72 h), ticks with `y_mask` (observed, not censored, valid). Baseline: max(0, train mean watering interval 12.6 h minus time since last event). Constant reference: train median of y = 0 h.

| Split | Ticks subset | Ticks | Segments | Baseline coverage | MAE baseline (h) | MAE constant (h) | Share y = 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| train | all y_mask | 7,598 | 23 | 16.8 % | 3.648 | 0.180 | 93.9 % |
| train | m above T | 465 | 16 | 98.1 % | 6.127 | 2.949 | 0 |
| val | all y_mask | 2,021 | 16 | 37.0 % | 3.457 | 0.197 | 94.7 % |
| val | m above T | 108 | 9 | 91.7 % | 5.450 | 3.690 | 0 |
| test | all y_mask | 2,391 | 15 | 41.8 % | 3.264 | 2.121 | 79.5 % |
| test | m above T | 489 | 11 | 73.8 % | 5.265 | 10.373 | 0 |

Coverage = share of ticks where the baseline exists (it is undefined before the first event of a segment). MAE is only over covered ticks. No tick had a 72 h cap (share 0).

## 3. Model B (event detection, FR-E02 wetting): rule detector, A-032 labels
Detector: the device rule (`features.EventTracker`: rise >= 10 points within 2 ticks, refractory). Scored on usable ticks only; a prediction within +-1 tick of a positive is a TP; predictions on ignore ticks are dropped.

| Split | Positives (valve/rain, no sector 5) | Found | TP | FP | Recall | Precision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| train | 178 | 178 | 178 | 0 | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| val | 32 | 32 | 32 | 1 | 1.00 | 0.97 |
| test | 40 | 40 | 40 | 0 | 1.00 | 1.00 |

FR-E02 claims cover **wetting and sensor_fault only**. `sensor_fault`: labels exist only as **synthetic** injected faults (none are in this real-data run) and no fault detector exists yet, so **no sensor_fault metric is reported**. Fast-drying and waterlogged are weak labels and are not evaluated or claimed.

## 4. What the numbers do and do not show
- **Event detection is circular.** The positives come from the same rise rule (+10 points in 2 ticks) that the detector uses, combined with a valve/rain signal. Recall 1.00 shows the on-device code reproduces the labeller (a useful port-consistency check), not that it detects wetting independently. Precision shows only that nearly every rule-detected rise on usable ticks is explained by valve or rain; rises without such a signal were removed by the ignore mask, so false alarms on unexplained wetting are not measured here.
- **Few positives.** val has 32 and test 40 events (train 178), from a few segments: any per-split number is noisy and carries no confidence interval.
- **Model A is dominated by "already dry".** 94 % (train, val) and 80 % (test) of usable ticks are already below T (y = 0), so the all-ticks MAE mostly measures that. On the meaningful ticks (moisture above T) the average-interval baseline has MAE 5.3 to 6.1 h and is **worse than the constant 0 h on train and val, better than it only on test** (where the constant gives 10.4 h). The baseline does not look strong; a model must beat it on the not-yet-dry ticks, and this evidence does not yet show it can.
- Coverage of the baseline is low overall (17 to 42 %) because most segments never show an event first.
- One site, one season, drip-irrigated farm, not household pots; the 12.6 h interval is a farm interval. Nothing here is a claim about the product's accuracy. Model training and a stronger threshold analysis stay for the next 4.AI2 slices.
