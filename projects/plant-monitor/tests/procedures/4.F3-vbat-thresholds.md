# Bench procedure: battery thresholds (task 4.F3, for Arsany / bench owner)

Purpose: replace the ASSUMED battery values in `firmware/components/power/include/nw_sleep.h` with measured ones.
Values to check: low-battery event at 3.4 V with clear at 3.5 V (FR-D06, A-008.2, A-018); Wi-Fi cutoff at 3.3 V (design proposal).
Not run yet. Needs a board with the sleep firmware flashed (not in this slice).

## Equipment
- Bench supply set to 1S-style voltages (current limit 0.2 A), or a cell from the BOM with the meter in series-free mode.
- Digital multimeter (DC volts, 0.1 mV or 1 mV resolution) on the cell terminal, and a second meter or scope on the 3V3 rail.
- USB serial log from the board (`vbat_mv` per wake).
- Power the board from the cell path, not USB: USB power gives a divider reading that is not a battery reading
  (`v1/WIRING.md` section 2.7). Keep J1 closed only after the section 2.3 steps.

## Steps
1. Supply at 3.60 V. Wait for two wakes. Record `vbat_mv` from the board and the DMM reading.
2. Lower in 50 mV steps to 3.30 V. At each step wait for two wakes and record both readings.
3. Acceptance: board `vbat_mv` within +/-50 mV of the DMM **[proposed tolerance, not agreed]**.
4. At the falling crossing of 3.40 V: the low-battery event fires once (serial log shows one event). It must not re-fire between 3.40 and 3.50 V.
5. Rising back through 3.50 V: the low state clears.
6. At 3.30 V with a report due (force `last_report_unix` old or wait for the hour): no Wi-Fi start in the log (cutoff holds).
7. Wi-Fi TX check with the supply at 3.35 V: record the minimum 3V3 rail during a report. If it drops below 3.0 V,
   the cutoff must go up. Record the value.

## Record
Write the measured offset, the crossing voltages and the 3V3 minimum into the Agent Log and open a PR that updates the
three `ASSUMED` markers in `nw_sleep.h` with the measured values. Never change a threshold without the measurement.
