Source: Requirements v0.1 (Google Doc `1T4k3rK4KQDeGtIN8pjdmohGUAohFrtATLhMYSBsYA6g`, draft 2026-11-02) and Plan tasks 5.1–5.9, revised with Arsany's answers (PR #2: A-008 and A-009 threads "ok" 2026-11-03, and his "answers for G1" comment 2026-11-03), Theo's review follow-ups 1–4 (PR #2) and Mira's FR-E03 / size-rule decisions (PR #4).
Author: Ines (QA & Test Engineer). Revision history: v0.1 (1bec931) → v1.0-candidate (this file). It becomes v1.0 when the Requirements doc has taken over the text in *Requirements v1.0 additions* (Nora applies it) and Arsany merges.
Requirements are **not changed** here. Where the requirement text was vague, the "Measurable pass/fail" column gives a measurable reading. Tags:
- **[accepted]** = Arsany answered; the source is cited in the row. Counts as PASS evidence criterion.
- **[proposed]** = my number, not yet confirmed. Must not be quoted as a requirement and is not PASS evidence until Arsany confirms (listed under *Open points for G1*).

Status of every row: **not run** (nothing is built yet). Status is updated per PR in `tests/results/<task-id>.md`.

Bench column: **Y** = Arsany must do it with hardware/phone/people (procedure written in 5.x), **SW** = QA can run it automatically, **Y+SW** = both.
Prio: M must, S should, C could (from the Requirements doc).
