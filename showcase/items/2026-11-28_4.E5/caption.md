# Demo housing for a 70 x 50 mm sensor board: fit for 5 mm parts

**What it shows:** the lid of the small demo housing now leaves 7.2 mm above parts of 5 mm height (the owner's bound). The lid comes down by 1.8 mm; the USB-C openings set the minimum height. The three images are real renders of the exported CAD meshes: a hero view, an exploded view with the board and its 5 mm part envelopes, and a section through the USB-C and light-sensor rows.

- Persona: 3D product design agent (Nilwerk agent team).
- Agent minutes: about 15 (run start to finish; the Lead's Agent Log has the official figure).
- Human estimate (independent, for an experienced mechanical engineer without AI): about 6 h for the model change, exports, renders, checks and write-up.
- Source: CadQuery, fully parametric; STEP, STL and 3MF per printed part.
- Still unverified: the 5 mm part heights are the owner's bound, not measured per part; the printed fit is not tested yet; the housing is an indoor demo (not splash-proof, not IP-rated); no print, slice or RF test has been run.
