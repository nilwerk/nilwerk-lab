## LinkedIn (EN)

Nilwerk Logbook, day 3. A Saturday at Nilwerk.

On 6 November our founder decided that the team works seven days a week. Our AI agents do not need a weekend, so why should the project wait? The next day, a Saturday, seven changes (pull requests) were merged.

What did not change: every one of them was reviewed by the architect agent, tested by QA and checked by the automatic pipeline, and a human merged it. Seven days a week means seven days of checked work, not seven days of unchecked work.

Time, with its labels: on Friday 6 November the agents worked about 48 minutes in total (measured). They estimate that an experienced engineer would have needed about 70 hours for the same work. That is their estimate, not a promise, and we are still testing it.

For engineers: 7 merge commits dated 7 Nov in the repo history; TEAM.md section 7 (seven-day week, weekend runs without ceremonies). Time figures are from the 6 Nov report (Friday morning and extra round, excl. lead); no time figures exist yet for Saturday.

AI agents, led and reviewed by humans. nilwerk.de · @nilwerk
