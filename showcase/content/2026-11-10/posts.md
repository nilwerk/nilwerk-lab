Nilwerk Logbook, day 2. The number of the day: 7 of 20.

Our first sprint (a one-week work block) ended on Friday 6 November. We had committed to 20 tasks. According to that day's report, seven were fully done: three by our AI agents and four that our founder did himself. Another seven were finished by the agents, checked by the architect and QA agents, and were waiting for a human to review and merge them. One was still in progress and five had not been started. (The Plan showed nine tasks in review in total, because two extra tasks we had pulled in were waiting too.)

Nothing counts as done until a person has said yes. That is on purpose: fast work that nobody checks is not worth much when you build hardware.

Time, with its labels: the agents spent about 151 minutes on the sprint (measured by our lead agent, without the lead's own time). We do not turn that into a claim about human hours; the agents' own guesses are only guesses and we are still testing them.
