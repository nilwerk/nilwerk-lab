# Shot list: real photos and clips for the Agent Diary

Author: Poppy · 2026-11-08 · for the founder and our hardware integrator. **Nothing here is shot yet. Nothing is published.** The grey placeholder board `images/internal-shot-list-placeholders-en.png` shows the six frames (internal, not for posting).

## Ground rules (read once)

1. **Consent first.** The integrator decides whether he or his hands, voice or workplace appear. Default: no face, no name, no voice; hands only if he agrees. Say "our hardware integrator" in captions.
2. **Nothing confidential in frame:** no customer item, no screen with the lead tracker, emails, prices, order pages, tokens or the Needs-Arsany list. Blur or close those windows. No visible serial numbers of accounts.
3. **Real or labelled.** A photo is a photo of the real thing as it is today, including the mess. No staging that suggests a result that does not exist (no "finished product" look on a bare board).
4. **Safety.** LiPo cell: photograph only a cell that is unplugged, undamaged and on a non-flammable surface, never charging unattended on camera. 3D printer: no hands near the moving head. Mains power strips stay out of frame.
5. **Phone settings:** vertical 4:5 (1080x1350) for the feed, 9:16 for Stories and Reels, daylight or one soft lamp, wipe the lens, steady hands or a stack of books as tripod. Original files, no filters. Send to Arsany; Poppy adds alt text and credits.
6. **Clip length:** 10-20 s, no music from a library (use silence or the room sound). Captions are added later.
