Fonts: self-host, no external requests

The fonts are in ./fonts (woff2, latin subset; Space Grotesk 700, Inter 400/600, JetBrains Mono 400/600) and loaded with @font-face in components.css. All three are SIL Open Font License 1.1 (licence texts are in the same folder), so self-hosting and commercial use are allowed. nilwerk.de promises no external fonts: keep it that way, do not use the Google Fonts CDN link on the website.
