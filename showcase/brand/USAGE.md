# Brand usage matrix (v0.1 brand, proposal pending Arsany's approval)

One page: which files to use for which use case and audience. Rules come from `brand-kit.md` (§5b two-layer pattern: plain story first, marked "For engineers" layer second; status chips; claim-check). Tokens: `brand.css` + `brand-status.css` (checked by `check_contrast.py`, `check_status_contrast.py`).

**Audiences:** **A** = non-technical (founders, SME owners, plant lovers, buyers of hardware projects): plain words, one idea, benefit first, a term of art gets a one-line explanation (`glossary.md`). **B** = technical (engineers, CTOs, makers): numbers, formulas, sources, what is not yet measured.

**Always:** AI agents, led and reviewed by humans (one wording). Calculated, simulated, measured and estimated are never mixed. No prices, offers, customer or lead content, no private repo URLs, no names of people without their OK. AI-generated imagery is labelled; code-generated images are not.

| Use case | A: non-technical | B: technical |
|---|---|---|
| **Social media** (Instagram, LinkedIn, stories, carousels) | **Files:** `templates/post-portrait-1080x1350.svg`, `post-square-1080x1080.svg`, `post-linkedin-1200x627.svg`, `story-1080x1920.svg`, `carousel-1080x1350.svg`; build with `build_post.py` / `build_extra.py`; texts in `content/<date>/posts.md`. **Tone:** warm British English (German: plain, "Sie"), short punchy lines, headline + intro lead. **Include:** artefact first, one number, honesty chip. **Avoid:** jargon, hype, superlatives. | **Files:** same templates, FOR ENGINEERS block and `claim-check.md`. **Tone:** precise, sourced. **Include:** formula, test, source file, what is stubbed or unmeasured. **Avoid:** unsourced numbers, "X times faster", unmarked estimates. |
| **Demo / presentation** (customer meetings, 16:9) | **Files:** `slides/templates/slide-1-title`, `-2-section`, `-6-closing`, `-3-key-number` (headline side); backgrounds `slides/png-clean/` + `slides/README.md` for Google Slides. **Tone:** plain, confident, "Sie" in German. **Include:** what it does for them, one proof number, next step. **Avoid:** acronyms without explanation, internal IDs. | **Files:** `slide-4-two-column` (right column), `slide-5-architecture`, `slide-3` engineers' lines. **Tone:** exact. **Include:** architecture, numbers with status chip, limits. **Avoid:** marketing claims, prices on shared decks. |

No tracking pixels, no attachments in the signature, no price or offer line. |
