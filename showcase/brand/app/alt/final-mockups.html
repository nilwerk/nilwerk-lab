Plant Monitor: final alternative design

All screens, the user journeys between them, and the back behaviour. Mock data only: plant names, numbers and prices are invented, and photos are gradient tiles, not images. Design only; nothing here is built or tested on a device. Spec in ALT-DESIGN.md and ALT-SCREENS.md.

1. User journey map

Arrows show forward moves. Back behaviour is the same everywhere: the system back and the on-screen back go to the previous screen in the stack. Sheets close first.

A. First-time setup

Goal: add a sensor and a plant in under 3 minutes (target, NFR-03).

1
Welcome

Read three benefits, tap Get started.

Back: none (first screen)
2
Sign up

Email and password, inline errors.

Back: Welcome
3
Setup 1–5

Find sensor, code, Wi-Fi, name and link, photo and details.

Back: previous step. Step 1 back: Plants. Setup is never a dead end.
4
Done

Tap Go to my plants.

Back: none (stack reset to Plants)

B. Daily check

Goal: see which plant needs water and act.

1
Plants (home)

The "needs water" card names the first plant.

2
Plant detail

Read the hero number and the next step, then water.

Back: Plants, same scroll row
3
Alerts (tab)

Badge shows open alerts. Filter by type.

4
Tap an alert

Opens the plant.

Back: Alerts, not Plants

C. Photo a plant

Goal: keep a dated photo history per plant.

1
Plant detail

Tap the thumbnail or Photos (n).

2
Gallery

Newest or Oldest. Grid with day headers. Tap a photo.

Back: plant detail
3
Photo viewer

Previous and Next buttons, swipe, pinch to zoom.

Back: grid, same position
4
Add photo

Picker: camera or library. Upload shows progress, then Done or Retry.

D. Account and Premium

Goal: see the plan and price, manage the account.

1

Grouped sections. Plan price shown before Premium.

2
Premium sheet

Back: sheet closes first, then Profile

Delete account

Back: Profile (nothing is deleted until confirmed)

2. All screens

Every phone is the same frame. Tabs: Plants, Alerts, Profile, shown only on the three tab roots and their sub-screens. Setup, gallery, viewer and sheets hide them.

3. Back, scroll and photos at a glance

Back

↩
System back

Closes the top sheet first, then pops the stack.

↩
On-screen back

Same as system back, in a fixed header.

↩
Plants root

First press: "Press back again to leave". Second press within 2 s exits.

Scroll

↕
Padding

Last row always clears the tab bar and the footer button.

↕
Lists

Virtualised. Pull to refresh; the Refresh button stays for the bench.

↕
Position

Kept when returning from detail to the list.

Photos

▦
Grid

Three per row, day headers, date on each photo.

▦
Upload

Queued offline. Progress, then Done or Retry.

▦
Picker

Approved: expo-image-picker. Added in the build PR, not here.
