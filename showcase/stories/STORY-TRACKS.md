# Two story tracks, one set of facts

Every true thing the team does can be told twice. Same facts (the canon in `origin/README.md`, the Agent Log,
merged PRs), two ways of telling them. Poppy plans both every week.

| | **Builder track** (technical) | **Everyone track** (non-technical) |
|---|---|---|
| For | Founders, engineers, product people who will judge the work | Anyone scrolling: curious people, future customers' colleagues, friends of friends |
| Hook | A result and a number: "v2: 150–226 days on one charge (simulated)" | A feeling or a question: "Can a team of AI coworkers keep a basil plant alive?" |
| Hero | The artefact: render, PCB, pipeline, PR | A character: **Basil**, the plant the team is building the sensor for, and the team members |
| Words | PCB, LiPo, PR, CI, edge AI | "the tiny brain", "the battery", "checked by three colleagues", "learns on the device" |
| Numbers | Exact, with units and sources | Few, rounded, human: "about half a year on one charge", "one hour of the boss's time" |
| Format | Carousel with specs, LinkedIn text, case study | Reel with captions, simple carousel, stories with polls and quizzes |
| Ask | "Building a connected device? nilwerk.de" | "Follow Basil" / "Help us name it" / "Ask Poppy anything" |

## Rules for the Everyone track
- **Same truth, simpler words.** Never round a simulation into a promise ("should last about half a year, says our
  simulation"), never drop the human checks, never invent events. Basil is a playful character, and posts say so.
- **One idea per slide, one feeling per Reel.** If a slide needs two sentences, it's two slides.
- **Captions on screen.** Most people watch muted. Every Reel carries its story in big text.
- **People, not tools.** Show who did it (Nora planned, Theo checked) rather than which tool ran.
- **Always one question back** to the viewer: a poll, a quiz, "what would you build?". Engagement is a conversation.
- **Glossary on request.** Each Everyone post links to the matching Builder post for people who want the details.

## Translation table (keep growing it)
| Builder word | Everyone words |
|---|---|
| PCB | the tiny brain / the circuit board |
| Enclosure, 3D model | its body / the case |
| LiPo + USB-C | a rechargeable battery, charged like a phone |
| Capacitive soil probe | the part that feels if the soil is dry |
| Pull request (PR) | a piece of work handed in for checking |
| Architect review → QA → pipeline | checked by three colleagues before it counts |
| Merge | the boss signs it off |
| Sprint | a one-week plan |
| Simulation | a test on the computer before we build it for real |
| Edge AI | it learns on the device itself, no cloud needed |
| Agent-minutes / human-hour estimate | "the team worked for about 1.5 hours; they reckon a person would need weeks" (only with "they reckon") |

## Recurring formats
1. **Who's looking after Basil?** (weekly Reel + carousel) The main series. Basil follows her sensor from idea to her pot.
   Ep. 1 = the origin story. Next big moments: first printed case, first real reading, first "I'm thirsty" message.
2. **Meet the team** (one agent a week, story or carousel). Avatar, job, favourite question, one thing they did this week.
3. **Guess what this is** (story with quiz). A close crop of a render or board, reveal the next day.
4. **Before / after** (Reel or carousel). v1 → v2, wireframe → screen, sketch → print.
5. **Ask Poppy** (stories question box). Answer one question a week in plain words.
