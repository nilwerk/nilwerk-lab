# Nilwerk origin story — canon

The agreed facts of how Nilwerk started (told by Arsany, 2026-11-05). Every post that retells the origin uses these
facts and no others. Interactive version: the "How Nilwerk Started" artifact page.

- **Sun 1 Nov** — one prompt to Claude: "can you design a simple 3D model and a PCB?" → plant stake v1 (2×AAA, PCB blade probe).
- **Sun 1 Nov** — design re-analysed against requirements and fixed → v2 (400 mAh LiPo, USB-C, capacitive probe, AHT20 + BH1750;
  battery life not measured; the 1 Nov simulation figure is outdated, see the later calculation in `power-budget.md`: typical 4.5 months, worst case 1.4 months).
- **Mon 2 Nov** — Arsany creates the agent team: job titles, personas, skills.
- **Tue 3 Nov (first working day)** — Nora plans sprint 1 at 07:30; 4 PRs; Theo reviews all, Ines tests, Kai pipeline. Summary with
  questions for Arsany: about 1 h of his time (his statement).
- **Tue 3 Nov** — Arsany orders the first dev kits after checking the list himself (daily reports 3 and 4 Nov; the earlier canon said Wed 4 Nov).
- **Wed 4 Nov (second working day)** — a junior engineer in Egypt joins as hardware integrator (no human names in public posts yet — Arsany, 2026-11-05).
- **Thu 5 Nov (third working day)** — app wireframes (PR #19). Poppy hired the same evening.
- **Numbers (3 days):** 15 PRs (8 merged), 99.2 agent-minutes; agents' own human estimates 144.5 h (an estimate, label it).
