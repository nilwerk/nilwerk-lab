Can a team of AI coworkers keep a basil plant alive?

That's the question behind our new series. On Sunday our founder asked an AI to design a little sensor that feels when the soil is dry. By Monday he had a team: 10 AI coworkers with jobs and personalities, and two humans who check everything.

Three days in, the team has designs for the case and the circuit board, a rechargeable battery that should last about half a year (according to our simulation) and the first sketch of an app.
